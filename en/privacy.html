Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-05 (previous versions: 2026-10-21, 2026-10-15)

Applies to: Stellar Bloom (행성 키우기), Wizard Tower (마법사의 탑) and other mobile games published by Starlit Camp

1. Overview

Starlit Camp ("we") respects your privacy and complies with applicable laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea, and Google Play policies. Our games can be played without an account or login, and we do not collect directly identifying information such as your name, email or phone number inside the game.

These providers may process information on servers outside your country, including the United States.

4. Children

Our games are suitable for all ages, including children. Before any ad is requested, the game asks for the player's age. For players under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in their country) we:

  • do not show personalized (interest-based) ads, and request only non-personalized, child-appropriate ads;
  • pass child-directed treatment signals (COPPA / child flags) to our ad partners;
  • do not use the advertising ID for personalized advertising or cross-app tracking.

Parents may contact us to review or delete information related to their child.

5. Your choices and rights

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all game data stored on your device.
  • To request access, correction or deletion, contact us. We will respond promptly and forward requests to the relevant third-party services where needed.

6. Retention

We do not store personal information ourselves. Third-party retention follows each provider's policy; Firebase Analytics data retention is set to 14 months.

7. Security

Communication with third-party services is encrypted (HTTPS), and we keep the information we handle to a minimum.

8. Changes

We will post any changes on this page with a new effective date.
