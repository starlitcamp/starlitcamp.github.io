A wizard stands atop a small tower in a field at sunset. Hover your cursor over the rocks and crystals floating in the sky to chip them away, and the tower rises floor by floor with what you gather. Hold off the monsters flying in from both sides and climb through clouds, storms and auroras all the way to the sea of stars.

Features

  • Chip, smash, repeatJust hover your mouse: your cursor strikes every resource and monster in range at once. A ring fills around it to show when the next strike lands, and upgrades make it faster and wider.
  • Floor by floor, to the starsWhat you gather raises the tower one floor at a time. From the sunset fields through the clouds, storms and auroras to the sea of stars, every height brings new scenery, resources and monsters.
  • Defend the towerMonsters fly at the top of the tower from both sides. Your wizard fires magic bolts on his own, and a giant guardian arrives every 10 floors.
  • When do you seal it?Seal the tower at any time to keep every star shard you've earned. Let the barrier fall and you keep only half. Push higher or stop now? That's the call every run.
  • A constellation legacySpend star shards on a constellation map in the night sky to make your next tower stronger. Four branches: gathering, tower, barrier and magic, plus blueprints for special facilities every 10 floors.
