Climb a tower crawling with monsters, one floor at a time. Defeat the guardian every 10 floors to rise from a 1st-circle apprentice to a 10th-circle archmage, and sweep away endless hordes with spells that evolve as you level them up in this pixel-art idle RPG.

Features

  • From apprentice to archmageDefeat the guardian blocking the way every 10 floors to raise your wizard circle, and each circle unlocks new spells. Every 10 floors the tower walls change color and shape, with a ring of starlight the first time you get there. Fire, ice, lightning and arcane, plus wind, earth, light and darkness awakening in each dimension: 25 spells in all.
  • Spells that evolve and combineLevel up and a fireball becomes a fire blast, until at last meteors rain down. Grow two elements to discover combos like Steam Burst, Plasma Beam and Hellfire, then pick branches to craft your own build.
  • Swarming monster hordesShield bearers, healing priests, ghosts, giant ogres and elite monsters. A guardian waits every 5 floors, every 10 floors a horde raid strikes, and every monster you defeat showers you with gold.
  • Climb, ascend, repeatGrow your tower with sealed rooms and 9 timed research projects, then reach the Starlit Stairs to ascend and take on the tower of a new dimension. Gold keeps piling up while you're away (open the Starlight chest when you return, or watch an ad for double), and research continues even when the game is closed.
  • Familiar expeditions and monster racesFrom the 2nd circle, send familiars on 1, 4 or 8-hour expeditions to the floors you've reached and bring back gold and mana crystals, sometimes with a rare map. From the 3rd circle, a monster race starts every 30 minutes: cheer for the monster you think will win (it's free), earn gold if you're right, and a winning streak raises the reward.
