Wizard Tower

Android · Coming soonRecruiting testersClosed testingOut now on Google Play

Climb a tower crawling with monsters, one floor at a time. Sweep away endless hordes with spells that evolve as you level them up in this pixel-art idle RPG.

How to join the test Get it on Google Play

Features

  • 25 evolving spellsFire, ice, lightning and arcane, plus wind, earth, light and darkness awakening in each new dimension. A fireball becomes a fire blast, and at last meteors rain down.
  • Combo spells and branchesLevel up two elements to discover combos like Steam Burst, Plasma Beam and Hellfire, then pick branches to craft your own build.
  • Swarming monster hordesShield bearers, healing priests, ghosts, giant ogres and elite monsters. A guardian waits every 5 floors, and every 10 floors a horde raid strikes: bat storms, splitting floods, giant marches and more.
  • Climb, ascend, repeatUnseal rooms to grow your tower, then reach the Starlit Stairs to ascend and take on the tower of a new dimension. Gold keeps piling up while you're away, and research continues even when the game is closed.

Join the closed test

Wizard Tower is in closed testing on Google Play. Anyone can join in four steps:

  1. Join the tester groupSign in with the Google account you use on the Play Store, open the Google Group Starlit Camp Testers and click Join group.
    Join the group
  2. Opt in to the testOpen the test link and tap Become a tester. Right after joining the group, it can take a few minutes to take effect.
    Open the test link
  3. Install from Google PlayOnce you've opted in, install the game from its Google Play page. You can also search for it in the Play Store app on your phone.
    Get it on Google Play
  4. Keep it for 14 days and send feedbackTo qualify for public release, testers need to keep the app installed for at least 14 days. Play now and then, and send us bugs or ideas by email or through private feedback on the Play Store. It helps a lot.
    Send feedback

※ Android only. Use the same Google account to join the group, opt in and install. Content and balance may change during the test.
