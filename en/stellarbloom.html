Stellar Bloom

Android · Coming soonRecruiting testersClosed testingOut now on Google Play

A speck of dust in a nebula. Pull in the matter around you and grow into a planet, a star, a black hole and finally a galaxy.

How to join the test Get it on Google Play

Features

  • Evolve through 24 celestial bodiesProtoplanets, super-Earths, brown dwarfs, red supergiants, magnetars, supermassive black holes and elliptical galaxies. Each stage grows from Lv.1 to Lv.5.
  • Something is coming. Turn the tables.Near the end of every run, something rises out of the depths. Outgrow it before it arrives and you swallow it instead, and the run goes into overtime with something bigger on the way.
  • Beyond the event horizon: PHASE 2The moment you first become a black hole, the screen tears open and PHASE 2 begins. The Observer's voice and 12 observation logs reveal the hidden story.
  • What you swallow shapes your fateYour evolution branches depending on whether you absorbed mostly ice, gas or heavy elements.
  • Living gravityDrifting bodies pull on each other too, and when two collide, the bigger one swallows the smaller.
  • Real astronomyEvery new body you discover adds a short, science-based entry to your codex.

Join the closed test

Stellar Bloom is in closed testing on Google Play. Anyone can join in four steps:

  1. Join the tester groupSign in with the Google account you use on the Play Store, open the Google Group Starlit Camp Testers and click Join group.
    Join the group
  2. Opt in to the testOpen the test link and tap Become a tester. Right after joining the group, it can take a few minutes to take effect.
    Open the test link
  3. Install from Google PlayOnce you've opted in, install the game from its Google Play page. You can also search for it in the Play Store app on your phone.
    Get it on Google Play
  4. Keep it for 14 days and send feedbackTo qualify for public release, testers need to keep the app installed for at least 14 days. Play now and then, and send us bugs or ideas by email or through private feedback on the Play Store. It helps a lot.
    Send feedback

※ Android only. Use the same Google account to join the group, opt in and install. Content and balance may change during the test.
