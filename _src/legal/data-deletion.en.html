1. Game data on your device

Our games have no accounts; game progress is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings > Apps > (game name) > Storage > Clear data, deletes it immediately.

2. Data processed by advertising and analytics partners

  1. Email starlitcampgames@gmail.com with the subject "[Game name] data deletion request" (e.g. "[Wizard Tower] data deletion request").
  2. Including your device's advertising ID (Android Settings > Privacy > Ads) helps us process the request. Resetting or deleting your advertising ID also unlinks past records.
  3. We process requests within 30 days and forward them to the relevant partners, such as Unity (LevelPlay / Unity Ads) and Google (Firebase).

3. What is deleted and what may be kept

  • Deleted: app usage, ad interaction, diagnostics and crash records linked to your device identifiers.
  • Minimal records required by law or for fraud prevention may be retained for a limited time under each partner's policy.
