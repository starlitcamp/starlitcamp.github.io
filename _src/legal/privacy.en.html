1. Overview

Starlit Camp ("we") respects your privacy and complies with applicable laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea, and Google Play policies. Our games can be played without an account or login, and we do not collect directly identifying information such as your name, email or phone number inside the game. To keep your progress safe, the game automatically backs up your progress to the cloud under a random account ID, and you can link a Google Play Games account to continue on another device.

4. Children

Our games are suitable for all ages, including children. Before any ad is requested, the game asks for the player's age. For players under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in their country) we:

  • do not show personalized (interest-based) ads, and request only non-personalized, child-appropriate ads;
  • pass child-directed treatment signals (COPPA / child flags) to our ad partners;
  • do not use the advertising ID for personalized advertising or cross-app tracking.

Parents may contact us to review or delete information related to their child.

5. Your choices and rights

  • In the game's Settings > Delete account you can immediately delete your cloud save and game account. You can turn promotional notifications off in Settings at any time.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all game data stored on your device.
  • To request access, correction or deletion, contact us. We will respond promptly and forward requests to the relevant third-party services where needed.

6. Retention

We do not store personal information ourselves. Third-party retention follows each provider's policy; Firebase Analytics data retention is set to 14 months. Cloud saves and game accounts are kept until you delete your account or ask us to, and are then deleted without delay.

7. Security

Communication with third-party services is encrypted (HTTPS), and we keep the information we handle to a minimum.
